About Bataan

Location

Bataan is a peninsula province in Central Luzon (Region III), bounded by the South China Sea to the west, Manila Bay to the east, and Zambales to the north. Its capital is Balanga City.

Background and History

Bataan gained global prominence during World War II as the site of the Fall of Bataan (April 1942) and the infamous Bataan Death March. Today, it honors this legacy while flourishing as an eco-tourism and heritage destination.

Culture and Traditions

Bataaeños are deeply rooted in Catholic faith and Filipino resilience. Their culture blends Tagalog and Kapampangan influences, featuring vibrant community fiestas and the annual Day of Valor (April 9).

Known Attractions
Mt. Samat National Shrine Las Casas Filipinas Pawikan Conservation Center Dunsulan Falls Sisiman Bay Bataan Death March Trail Anvaya Cove Granadero Fortress Morong Beach Balanga Cathedral
Why Visit Bataan?
1
Rich Historical Heritage

Walk the same ground as wartime heroes and visit shrines that bring WWII history to life.

2
Pristine Beaches & Coves

Bataan's coastline offers hidden beaches and stunning sunsets away from the crowds.

3
Eco-Adventure Awaits

Trek lush rainforests, discover waterfalls, and spot endangered sea turtles.
